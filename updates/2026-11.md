## Method

1. **Reference audit.** Every reference dated 2025 or 2026 (155 entries) was looked up on PubMed by DOI (or PMID) and its year, volume, issue and pages compared with the chapter entry. Pages were compared after expanding abbreviated ranges. All entries that already had final details matched PubMed.
2. **New-evidence search.** For each chapter, a topic query from `OUTLINE.md` was combined with a filter for systematic reviews, meta-analyses, RCTs, guidelines and consensus statements (publication type or title words). There were extra searches for chairside CAD/CAM, the virtual patient and jaw motion, and partial-coverage restorations. About 630 records were screened by title. 25 were shortlisted and their abstracts read in full (efetch). Every number added to the book was checked against the abstract.
3. Papers already cited in the book were skipped (for example Linn 2026, Zhu 2026, Prott 2026, Martins 2026, Bousaidi 2026, Anas 2026, Almutairi 2026 and dos Santos 2026, which were added while the book was being written).

## Top 5 items for the author to review

1. **Ch 14, Section 14.6.1: chipping conflict.** A meta-analysis of 14 RCTs found *less* chipping with veneered zirconia than with metal-ceramic (RR 0.59, low certainty) [19]. The text now presents this against the chapter's earlier narrative. Please confirm the wording ("not settled") and whether Key points or Chapter 8 need a matching note.
2. **Ch 11, Section 11.4.4 and Evidence Box: first RCT on printed definitive crowns** [17]. 78.6% vs 92.9% fracture-free survival at 1 year, and about six times more wear. Check that the Evidence Box wording ("long-term provisional or selected definitive") is still the stance you want.
3. **Ch 7, Section 7.8.3 and Table 7.3: AI design time.** "Faster" softened to "usually faster" because the newest meta-analysis found no significant pooled time saving [19]. One author of that review declared an honorarium from an AI design-software company.
4. **Renumbering in the notes files.** Adding references renumbered 9 chapters (for example, in Ch 17 and Ch 18 most numbers shifted by one). Notes written before this update use the old numbers; each notes file now has an old → new map. Decide whether you want the older notes rewritten to the new numbers.
5. **Window choice and the online-first backlog.** The window started at 2026-10-01 (scheduled task) rather than 2026-11-17 (procedure). 21 references are now online-first (14 old, 7 new) and need their final details at the next update.
