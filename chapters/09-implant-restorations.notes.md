## Weak evidence
- **Custom vs stock abutments:** two RCTs with conflicting results (Schepke 2017 null; Robaian 2025 positive).
- **Non-original Ti-bases (Stucki 2021; Strauss 2022):** one prospective cohort of 24 patients.
- **Emergence angle (Katafuchi 2018):** cross-sectional radiographic association, not causation.
- **Angled screw channels (Atieh 2025):** four studies, 167 crowns, short-term only.
