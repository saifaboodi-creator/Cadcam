## References to check
- **Refs 3–5, 16, 24, 25 (2025–2026):** recent. Confirm the final details.
- **Ref 8 (Robaian 2025, Dent J):** a single-centre RCT in a smaller journal, and the only positive trial for custom abutments here. It is presented with that caveat.
- **Ref 9 (Happe 2022, Int J Comput Dent):** PMID only. **Ref 21 (Abduo 2010):** PMID only.
- **Ref 25 (Marinkovic 2026, J Clin Exp Dent):** a lower-tier journal; all included studies were non-randomised and most had a moderate to critical risk of bias (stated).

## Weak evidence
- **Custom vs stock abutments:** two RCTs with conflicting results (Schepke 2017 null; Robaian 2025 positive).
- **Non-original Ti-bases (Stucki 2021; Strauss 2022):** one prospective cohort of 24 patients from one group, without a control group; the 5-year report found favourable long-term performance.
- **Emergence angle (Katafuchi 2018):** cross-sectional radiographic association, not causation.
- **Angled screw channels (Atieh 2025):** four studies, 167 crowns, short-term only.

## Caveats / uncited statements

- **9.5.2 ASC torque:** the uncited torque claim was removed; the bullet now says to follow the manufacturer's torque protocol (#20).
- **9.2.2 scan-body wear/autoclaving:** labelled expert opinion (#7).
- **9.6.1 "fit problems come mostly from data acquisition":** removed from the text and Figure 9.3 (#5).

- **9.6.3 design principles (cantilevers, cleansable intaglio):** expert synthesis, uncited.

## Decisions made
- I recommended avoiding non-original Ti-bases, based on Stucki 2021. Revised at the 2026-11-22 review (#2): "use with caution", with the 5-year favourable result (Strauss 2022).
- I adopted the German S3 guideline (Waltenberger 2025) as the material-selection anchor in 9.7.
