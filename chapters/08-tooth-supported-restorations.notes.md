- **Endocrowns (Sedrez-Porto 2016):** only three clinical trials (success 94–100%). Most of the data are in vitro.
- **Veneer incisal coverage (Albanesi 2016):** eight studies, with no RCTs comparing designs.
- **Zirconia survival (Sailer 2015; Pjetursson 2015):** mainly **veneered** zirconia. Monolithic zirconia is under-represented.
