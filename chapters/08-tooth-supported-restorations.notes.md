## Weak evidence
- **Endocrowns (Sedrez-Porto 2016):** only three clinical trials (success 94–100%). Most of the data are in vitro.
- **Veneer incisal coverage (Albanesi 2016):** eight studies, with no RCTs comparing designs.
- **Zirconia survival (Sailer 2015; Pjetursson 2015):** mainly **veneered** zirconia. Monolithic zirconia is under-represented.

## Caveats / uncited statements
- **Table 8.1 (typical minimum dimensions):** ranges commonly given in manufacturer instructions and teaching, **uncited**; the table title now says so (review #10). Check against current manufacturers' instructions for use, or cite them directly.
- **8.1.1 "rounded internal angles / bur radius" principle:** standard teaching, uncited (Chapter 10 now covers bur geometry with references).
- **8.6.3 connector guidance ("height matters more than width"):** general engineering principle, uncited; now labelled as such in the text (review #10). "Connectors are usually the weakest point" (8.6.1) and "thin areas raise the risk of fracture" (8.1.1) are labelled expert opinion; 8.1 "bonded partial-coverage… most conservative choice for posterior teeth" and 8.6.3 span limit for glass-ceramic FDPs are labelled expert opinion (review #3, #6, #7, #10).
