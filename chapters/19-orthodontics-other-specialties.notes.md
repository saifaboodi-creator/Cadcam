## Weak evidence
- **Clear aligners**: Rossini 2015 is older and heterogeneous; Haouili 2020 is a single-system prospective study of 38 patients. A recent high-quality systematic review of aligner predictability would strengthen 19.2.3.
- **Indirect bonding**: evidence is in vitro (ref 7) or single-author/single-clinic cohorts (refs 8, 9, the same author). No systematic review was found.
- **Guided endodontics**: in vitro studies (refs 12–14) plus one failure case report. No clinical comparative trial or systematic review was found in the searches. The search "guided endodontics accuracy systematic review" returned nothing.
- **Splints**: only a narrative review (ref 10). No SR comparing printed vs milled splints was found.
- **MADs**: ref 11 is a non-randomised practice-based study. Its finding that the conventional device performed better is reported with caveats.
- **Crown lengthening**: a single technique report (ref 18). No comparative clinical evidence.
- **VSP orthognathic**: certainty low/very low (ref 20). Refs 20 and 21 reach somewhat different conclusions (modest benefit vs no difference). Both are presented.
- **PSI and trauma**: refs 25 and 26 rest on case series/heterogeneous designs.
