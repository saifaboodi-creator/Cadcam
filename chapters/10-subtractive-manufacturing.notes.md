## Weak evidence
- Almost all evidence in this chapter is **in vitro** (fit, chipping, sintering, bur wear). No clinical trials compare milling machines, axes or milling modes.
- Machine axes (Section 10.2.2): the only cited support for "5-axis is most accurate" is a narrative review (ref 3). No comparative study of axis number and fit was found on PubMed.
- Speed sintering (Section 10.7.1): the studies disagree (ref 19 found better margins with high-speed sintering using a different, maximum-distance measurement method). This is presented as mixed evidence.
- Several sintering studies are in mid-tier journals with small samples (n = 10 per group).
