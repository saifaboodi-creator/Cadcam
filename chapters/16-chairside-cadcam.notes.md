- Chairside printing of definitive crowns: one in vitro study [12].
- Chairside economics: no dedicated chairside cost-effectiveness study found; [12] is in vitro and [20] is an implant digital-vs-conventional study (not chairside). Text states this explicitly.
- Long-term chairside survival rests mainly on single-practice case series with feldspathic ceramics [13–15]; acknowledged in an Evidence Box.
