## Weak evidence
- Chairside speed-sintered zirconia: only a case report [8] and in vitro studies [9,10]; no clinical survival data cited.
- Chairside printing of definitive crowns: one in vitro study [12].
- Chairside economics: no dedicated chairside cost-effectiveness study found; [12] is in vitro and [20] is an implant digital-vs-conventional study (not chairside). Text states this explicitly.
- Long-term chairside survival rests mainly on single-practice case series with feldspathic ceramics [13–15]; acknowledged in an Evidence Box.

## Caveats / uncited statements
- Table 16.1 (case selection) and Table 16.3 (staged introduction) are expert-opinion frameworks, not evidence-derived.
- Table 16.2 time budget is deliberately qualitative (no minutes given) because no reliable comparative timing data were verified.
- Section 16.4.1 descriptions of block types (leucite milled in final state; lithium disilicate milled partially crystallised then crystallised) are standard materials knowledge, uncited; detailed in Chapter 13.
- Section 16.6.1–16.6.2 cost drivers and savings are general reasoning, uncited.
- Delegation of tasks to assistants "within local regulations" — legal scope varies by country.
