## Weak evidence
- **Glazing vs polishing, and adjustment of zirconia (12.4–12.5):** almost entirely in vitro, on flat specimens with single products. The chapter says so in an Evidence Box.
- **Enamel wear (Ref 11):** an umbrella review of only four systematic reviews, with no meta-analysis possible.
- **Speed sintering (Refs 3–5):** all in vitro. There is no clinical outcome evidence.
- **Extrinsic stain durability (Ref 7):** a single in vitro study simulating about one year of brushing. Its authors say it failed to establish durability limits.
- **Ref 15 (Gaonkar 2020):** n = 5 per group.
- **Ref 16 (Kozmacs 2017):** a pilot study with 6 operators per group.

## Decisions made
- I followed the OUTLINE.md plan for Ch 12 (staining, glazing, polishing; digital and physical fit checks; troubleshooting) and added post-processing by material (12.2) and a QC routine (12.8), because they are needed to connect Chapters 10, 11 and 16.
- I gave no specific firing temperatures, times or polishing-kit brand recommendations. These are product-specific and change, so the reader is directed to the manufacturer's instructions.
- I deliberately left out a gingival shade-matching study I found (Sanchez de Martín 2026: a pilot, gingiva-only, students) as too weak and off-topic for tooth shade.
- I deliberately left out a triple-scan study of printed provisionals (Manchalwar 2025, J Indian Prosthodont Soc). Its reported marginal gaps (0.05 ± 0.02 µm) are almost certainly a unit error, so I could not cite its numbers reliably.
- Brand names appear only in the reference titles, following STYLE.md.
