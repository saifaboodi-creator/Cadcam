## Weak evidence
- **Ref 22 (Shah 2024 umbrella review)** and **Ref 21 (Ghaffari 2022)** are in lower-impact journals and summarise mostly in vitro wear data. Ref 22 rests on four systematic reviews with no meta-analysis; ref 21 on 16 articles.
- **Ref 24 (Ayedi 2025, J Contemp Dent Pract)** is a systematic review of 15 in vitro bond-strength studies in a lower-tier journal. Its conclusion matches Özcan 2015 (Ref 23) and Al-Bermani (Ref 25).
- **Ref 27 (Kern & Gläser 2023)** is a case report of three long-term cases (veneered alumina and zirconia). It's cited only for the abrasion protocol and as expert evidence of durability.
- **Ref 28 (Demir 2024)** is a single in vitro study (crowns not pre-abraded, self-adhesive cements) for the cleaning-agent recommendation; only the interaction of cleaner and cement was significant.
- **Ref 5** is about additively manufactured zirconia (two materials). I used it only for the phase–strength–translucency–ageing relationship, now stated as the result of one laboratory study.
- **Ref 14** rests on eight studies (three on veneered zirconia); the authors could identify only tendencies.
- There are no long-term (>10-year) clinical data on monolithic 4Y/5Y zirconia, and LTD under clinical conditions is weakly evidenced.

## Caveats / uncited statements (general textbook knowledge)
- The ~20–25% linear sintering shrinkage of pre-sintered zirconia (14.4.1) has no citation; marked [UNVERIFIED].
- The volume increase on t→m transformation is stated as "several percent" (14.2.1). The commonly cited value is ~3–5%. Not cited; marked [UNVERIFIED].
- That saliva phosphates block MDP bonding, and that phosphoric acid should not be used to clean zirconia (14.7.3), is widely taught but not cited; marked [UNVERIFIED]. Ozkan Ata 2025 (Am J Dent) was found but **not used**: its abstract reports implausible bond strengths ("1768 MPa"), probably a units error.
- Table 14.2's recommendation of conventional or self-adhesive cements for retentive preparations has no citation (the APA part is now cited to ref 24). Rubber dam is labelled expert opinion.
- The aggressive-abrasion caution for 4Y/5Y (Clinical Tip, 14.7.4) is expert opinion and now labelled as such. The parameters come from Kern & Gläser (ref 27; 50 µm, 0.25 MPa).
- Steam sterilisation and ageing (14.5.1, 14.5.3) is a general caution, not cited; the 14.5.1 sentence is marked [UNVERIFIED].
- "LTD has not emerged as a common clinical cause of failure" (14.5.3) is an expert judgement, now labelled "(expert opinion)".
- Table 14.3: "Long-span tooth-supported FDP — 3Y-TZP" is expert opinion; "Nest restorations so that connectors sit in the strongest layers" (Key Points, Lab Note) is an expert recommendation.

## Decisions made
- I cross-referred to Chapters 8, 9 and 13 for survival, implant and glass-ceramic content, and repeated only zirconia-specific findings.
- Ceramic implants are excluded (out of scope). Ref 14 is used only for its chipping data on veneered versus monolithic restorations.
- Printed zirconia is left to Chapter 11 and not discussed here (14.1 now points there).
- Brand names appear only in reference titles, per STYLE.md.
- Ref 20 (S3 guideline) is reused from Chapter 9 for the statement that only 3Y-TZP is supported for implant-supported short-span FDPs and full-arch reconstructions, and for micro-veneering.
