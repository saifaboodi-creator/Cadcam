## Weak evidence
- Printed "permanent" resin crowns/FDPs: evidence almost entirely in vitro; meta-analysis certainty low to very low (ref 15). Text says this explicitly (Evidence Box).
- Printed dentures: certainty of evidence mostly very low (ref 18); findings between reviews differ (ref 18: small retention advantage of printed over milled; ref 19: conventional more retentive than printed). Both reported.
- Printed zirconia: mostly in vitro; one 12-month RCT (Cai 2025, ref 25).
- Build orientation: findings are device- and resin-specific and partly conflicting (ref 7 favours horizontal for models; ref 8 found no effect of 0–20°).
