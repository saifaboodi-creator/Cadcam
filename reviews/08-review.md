## Discarded findings

1. **Ref 6 second author "Rebello de Sampaio FBW" should be "FB" (B22).** This is a false positive. PubMed's fore name is "F B W"; the initials field is truncated to two letters. "FBW" is correct.
2. **Ref 10 second author "Rosa WLO" should be "Rosa WL" (B23).** This is a false positive. PubMed's fore name is "Wellington Luiz de Oliveira da", so "WLO" is a correct rendering. It is not an error.

Sub-claims removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- B11 and C4 (#1): the point that the "glass-ceramics" were zirconia-reinforced lithium silicates. The abstract names only the products (Vita Suprinity, Celtra Duo) and calls them ceramics. "Glass-ceramics" is not wrong, and the material class cannot be confirmed from the abstract.
- B24 (#11): "single-operator" series. The abstract says only "a private practice".
- C11 (#13): the sentence "Veneered zirconia crowns were more often lost through fracture of the veneering ceramic". A crown lost through veneering-ceramic fracture must be veneered, so this inference is sound. Only "(mostly veneered at that time)", "(largely veneered)" and "veneered zirconia" in the recommendation remain in the finding. The fix for #13 still uses the abstract's term "densely sintered zirconia" for consistency.
- B21(f) (#10): 8.5.3, material choice by abutment colour. This is standard clinical guidance with no reference attached, and no factual error was found.

## Top items for the author

1. **#1 (major): 8.3.1 endocrown cement space.** The chapter says glass-ceramics "fitted best" at 60 µm and composites at 120 µm. The study found the best agreement between designed and measured gaps at these settings, not the best fit. It is also in vitro and needs a label.
