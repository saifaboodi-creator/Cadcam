## Discarded findings

1. **20.6.2 Tousidonis unit: "one of the first" and 2017–2025 period (A11, B16).** Not an error. Both checkers marked it as needing no change. The numbers 442, about 68%, 20% and 7% and the "eight-year period" are all in the abstract, and leaving out "one of the first" does not misstate the source.
2. **20.5.1 "Digital workflows are now part of routine practice, and graduates are expected to use them" uncited (B20).** This is a general framing statement with no factual error, and ref 14 supports it ("develop the next generation of technology-adept and practice-ready oral health professionals"). Adding a citation is an editorial choice. This follows the Chapter 16 decision on uncited standard statements.
3. **Reference 24 title says "complete denture" but the abstract describes removable partial dentures (A22, B12).** Not a chapter error. The reference reproduces the PubMed title exactly, the text follows the abstract (ref 25 also describes "removable partial denture fabrication scenarios"), and the notes file already flags it for a full-text check.

Sub-claims removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- A13, B7 and C6: that "with the ranking changing with arch type" is not in the ref 2 abstract. The abstract's clinical significance ("Different IOSs should be used according to the complete arch type") supports this paraphrase. Finding 5 keeps the complete-arch scope problem.
- A12: that the unit should be kg CO₂-eq. Ref 24's abstract uses "kgCO₂", and the chapter follows it.
- A14: that the Figure 20.2 note and 20.7.2 ("accuracy of merged datasets") break the trueness/precision rule. These describe registration error of merged datasets, not scanner trueness or precision, and repeat Chapter 6.
- B5 and C5: that "technician designs" misstates the comparator. The ref 20 abstract also refers to "experienced dental technicians"; the suggested fix in finding 4 uses the source's "human designs" anyway.

## Top items for the author

1. **#3 (major): 20.6.3 and Key Points, "responsibility stays with the professional [19]".** Ref 19 supports human oversight only; it lists responsibility as an open question. Keep the responsibility statement under the existing [UNVERIFIED] general principle.
2. **#2 (major): "patients prefer scanning for many indications [8]".** One RCT of 42 patients with tooth-supported crowns or FDPs measured discomfort, not preference.
3. **#4 (major): 20.7.1 AI design.** The review found faster design (not production), and lower acceptability for inlays and three-unit FDPs; "much faster" and "multi-unit FDPs" overstate it.
4. **#1 (major): 20.3.1 "more than twice as fast".** This is one of three RCTs in the review, which concluded that no recommendations can yet be given.
5. **#7 and #17 (minor, numbers): 18% cost reduction and LCA values.** The 18% is the cost-minimisation result (direct costs fell about 14%). The LCA values should be paired by laboratory (local 65.22 vs 77.29; global 78.63 vs 94.67 kg CO₂).
