## Discarded findings

1. **Reil bullet should state the model setting (A28).** The lead-in already says "Laboratory studies", and every number (2.24° vs 4.51°; 15:22 vs 9:35 min) matches the abstract. No error.
2. **Jorba-García mean differences may include in vitro data (A22).** The abstract does not say whether the −4.33° and −0.86° comparisons pooled clinical and model studies, and the chapter does not call them clinical. Not confirmable from the abstract; no error found.
3. **Table 17.2 lacks a Werny dCAIS row (A21).** The table shows a different review (ref 13) for dCAIS; the values do not conflict with the text or figure, and the caption warns against cross-review comparison. Editorial choice.
4. **Table 17.2 "pooled clinical means" not confirmed for ref 6 (B15).** Werny's PICO is "In patients receiving dental implants"; the abstract gives no sign that in vitro studies were pooled.
5. **Clinical Tip in 17.6.2 should be labelled expert opinion (A23).** Clinical Tip boxes are the STYLE.md template element for practical advice, and the tip already allows full occlusal loading "unless a protocol specifically supports" it, which is consistent with ref 22.
6. **Key Points "Robotics shows the smallest deviations, but on limited evidence" (A9).** Supported: ref 16 says robotic systems "consistently achieved superior accuracy over previously reported computer-assisted implant surgery (CAIS) methods", and the sentence already carries the limited-evidence caveat from ref 18.

Sub-claims removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- B23: "apex offsets about 0.3 mm" as a rounding error. The medians are 0.32–0.36 mm, so "about 0.3 mm" is acceptable.
- C16: the 17.3.4 sentence "the factors with the clearest evidence" is the author's synthesis pointing to sections that do cite evidence.
- C16: Table 17.3 "Research or high-volume centres | Robotics" is a suggested approach in a decision table; its rationale is cited correctly [16–18].
- C16: "vital structures such as the inferior alveolar nerve, adjacent roots and sinus floor" is standard anatomy, not a claim needing a reference.
- C20: refs 3 and 25 without a DOI. PubMed lists no DOI for either and the PMID is given, which is acceptable.
- C3 and B3: severity lowered from major to minor (findings 21 and 17). Ref 4 gives indirect support, and the 17.4.2 wording of ref 15 is already correct.

## Top items for the author

1. **#2 (major): 17.5.3 and Key Points, "no difference in postoperative pain".** Only the magnitude of pain did not differ. The duration of pain, speaking limitations and impact on routine activities did differ significantly between groups.
2. **#3 (major): 17.6.2 "Rotation is the main risk… non-indexed or round connections".** Ref 20 (10 typodonts, in vitro) neither ranks error sources nor tests connections. Key Points and Review Question 8 build on this.
3. **#1 (major): "prosthetically driven" cited to refs 1 and 4.** Neither abstract states it; ref 21 does. Fix the Key Points citation and the Figure 17.1 caption.
4. **#16 (minor): 2 mm safety margin.** Three meta-analyses recommend it (add ref 6), only one says "at least", and none covers robotics, so "with every technique" should be "with static and dynamic CAIS".
5. **#10 (minor): Figure 17.3 caption.** Body text and the bullet list on refs 7–9 have merged into the caption; split them and soften "confirm".
