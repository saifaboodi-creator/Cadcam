## Discarded findings

1. **Ref 18 author format "Cuevas-Suárez CE" (A13, C17; B note).** No error. PubMed indexes the name as "Enrique Cuevas-Suárez C" (fore name Carlos), and all three checkers agreed that the chapter's form is the conventional one.
2. **7.4.4 ref 4 "most used GANs or transformers"; add the 9-article sample (B13).** The abstract says "Primary network architectures include Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Transformers", which "most used" paraphrases fairly. Adding the sample size is an editorial choice, not an error.
3. **Uncited qualitative statements (A18 rest, B19, B20, C13 rest).** These are: Table 7.2 "Glass-ceramics need more thickness than zirconia", connector "Height matters more than width", "Too small risks binding", "Set to slight overlap"; 7.4.1 "earliest and still most common approach"; 7.1 Clinical Tip "Most laboratory platforms now allow this"; 7.7.2 "With average values, it identifies obvious interferences…"; 7.8 "AI design tools are now built into many CAD programs…". They are standard statements with no reference attached, and no factual error was found. The chapter points to Chapters 13–15 and Appendix A for material values (consistent with the Chapter 16 review).
4. **"Accuracy" in 7.8.2 ("less accurate proximal contacts") and 7.8.3 ("morphological accuracy") (A19 part, B21, C14 part).** Both follow the wording of the source (41368738: "proximal contact adaptation remained less accurate"; 42368773 and 42849515: "morphological accuracy") and do not describe scan accuracy, which is what the STYLE.md rule covers.

Sub-claims or ratings removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- B5: extending the implant-row problem to Table 7.1 and Key Points ("weaker for contacts"). This is supported by refs 16 and 17 (41875985: "refinement of proximal contacts… by experienced operators was recommended").
- B12: adding "only" to "170 caries-free lower first molars". The chapter already names the tooth type.
- B10: "best overall fit" for ref 7 is too broad. The abstract recommends 70 µm "for optimal marginal and internal fit".
- A21: Key Points bullet 5 infra-occlusion advice "sits in a bullet cited to [10,11]". The citation already stands after the first sentence only.
- C16: "listed below" contradiction and missing reference numbers in "Decisions made". The notes list "None" under UNVERIFIED items, and the update section explains the old numbering.
- A20(c), B22(b): the notes' "not marked inline, because Chapters 1–9 were frozen" conflicts with CLAUDE.md rule 2. This is a recorded author decision, not an error in the chapter; finding 5 offers expert-opinion labels instead.
- B7: rated major, and it calls ref 16 in vitro or in silico. It is merged into finding 7 as minor, because Key Points already says "the evidence is mostly in vitro". Ref 16 used data from 311 patients and is handled in finding 16.
- A4: rated major (training-set size). It is minor, because the total of 1,113 is correct for the dataset and the test results are correct.

## Top items for the author

1. **#1 (major): 7.7.1 bite-scan penetration cited to ref 10.** The systematic review does not mention penetration. Cite ref 10 for the factors it lists and mark the penetration sentence as expert opinion.
2. **#3 (major): 7.8.1 "Both performed less well… diastemas" [14].** Only the deep-learning program did. Its surface deviations were also larger, and the study was on typodonts.
3. **#4 (major): Table 7.3 implant row "proximal contacts weaker [15–17]".** Ref 15 found similar proximal contacts and ref 17 found comparable outcomes. Only ref 16 supports "weaker".
4. **#2 (major): 7.7.3 digital occlusal design.** Ref 12 compared impression techniques, not occlusal design. Say so in the text.
5. **#6 and #9 (minor): numbers and scope.** The margin model was trained on 1,035 scans, not 1,113. Ref 17's finding on FDPs applies to three-unit FDPs, not to multi-unit FDPs in general.
