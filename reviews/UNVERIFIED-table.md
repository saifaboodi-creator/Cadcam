# UNVERIFIED items in the book, with their paragraphs and conflict level

Prepared for the author on 2026-11-25. Source: the 39 inline **[UNVERIFIED]** marks in `chapters/*.md` (count matches `PROGRESS.md`) and the "UNVERIFIED items" lists in each chapter's `.notes.md` file. **No chapter text was changed.**

## How the conflict level was assigned

None of the 39 items contradicts its cited source. Each one is a claim that a PubMed abstract (or another available source) could not confirm. The level shows how much harm the claim would do if it turned out to be wrong.

- **Major:** the claim is (a) a clinical instruction or threshold that a reader may act on, or (b) a specific number stated as fact without a confirmed source.
- **Minor:** historical dates, legal or regulatory background, technical description, market statements, interpreting a figure from the wording, or a statement that evidence is absent. If these are wrong, the text needs correcting but clinical practice would not change.

**Totals:** 10 major and 29 minor (39 marks; 27 distinct claims, counting the shrinkage figure in Ch 10 and Ch 14 once).

## The 10 major items, grouped (what to fix first)

1. **Phosphoric acid and saliva contamination of zirconia** (Ch 14, #31–33, #35): 4 marks, one clinical rule. This is the most important item, because it is a bonding instruction repeated in the text, Table 14.2 and Key Points.
2. **120 µm marginal-gap threshold** (Ch 3, #13–14): the book uses it as the acceptance limit and Figure 4.2 refers to it. The full text of McLean & von Fraunhofer 1971 would settle it.
3. **20–25% sintering shrinkage** (Ch 10 #20, Ch 14 #29): the same number in two chapters, so one source fixes both.
4. **Digital bite-record tooth counts** (Ch 4, #16): a clinical recommendation that depends on how the abstract is read; the full text is needed.
5. **Steam sterilisation and ageing of zirconia** (Ch 14, #30): a handling caution.
