## Discarded findings

1. **9.2.1 "how design features affect accuracy [1]" breaks the trueness/precision rule (A25, C15).** Not an error. The sentence describes the topic of ref 1 in the source's own words ("ISB features/design, accuracy"), and no measurement is reported. Checker A itself suggested leaving it.
2. **9.3.3 "In one prospective study…" and "zirconia restorations" misdescribe ref 11 (C23).** A false positive. Ref 12, from the same group and on the same 24-patient design, is titled "…a prospective cohort study", and ref 11's own title says "Zirconia reconstructions cemented on non-original titanium bases".
3. **Table 9.3 complete-arch row: ref 24 excluded monolithic zirconia and does not support "screw-retained" (B26).** A false positive. Ref 24 excluded *fully* monolithic zirconia (no metal); its metal-zirconia arm is "Monolithic zirconia prostheses with a metallic substructure", and the review is limited to "screw-retained complete-arch prostheses". This matches "monolithic zirconia (with or without a metal substructure)". The separate try-in point is kept in finding 24.

Sub-claims removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- C16: "has become the workhorse of CAD/CAM implant prosthodontics" (9.3.3) is a descriptive metaphor, not a factual claim needing a reference. The rest of C16 is kept in findings 7, 14 and 20.
- B22: the list of clinical fit checks in 9.6.1 (one-screw test, radiographs, screw-resistance test, verification jig) is standard practice and is not attributed to ref 20. The rest of B22 is kept in finding 21.
- B24: the point that ref 22 "reports success rates, not chipping rates". Its abstract attributes the lower success of bilayered zirconia to veneer fracture, so it does support the chipping comparison.
- B24 and B26: the point that ref 24's metal-zirconia group "excluded fully monolithic zirconia" as a reason against the Table 9.3 wording (see discarded item 3).

## Top items for the author

1. **#3 (major): 9.4 sample size.** The emergence-angle percentages come from 168 implants in 83 patients, not 225 implants. 225 was the starting data set.
2. **#2 (major): non-original Ti-bases.** Ref 12 is a 5-year report from the same group on the same kind of 24-patient cohort, not independent evidence. It found that the angle association had gone by 5 years and that long-term performance was favourable. "Avoid non-original Ti-bases" in Key Points overstates one uncontrolled study of one product.
3. **#1 (major): 9.2.2 photogrammetry "more accurate".** Ref 5 does not compare photogrammetry with intraoral scanning. Ref 6's meta-analyses are in vitro only, and the chapter should use trueness and precision.
4. **#4 and #5 (major): two uncited factual claims.** Residual cement as a "well-recognised risk factor" (9.5.1), and "remaining fit problems come mostly from data acquisition" (9.6.1 and Fig 9.3). Neither has a source or an [UNVERIFIED] marker. Please decide whether the Chapter 1–9 freeze still prevents inline marking (finding 31).
5. **#28 and #29 (minor): Key Points on retention and chipping.** Key Points say screw retention has "fewer complications" [17,18], but ref 17 found more technical problems. They also cite ref 24 for chipping, which it does not report.
