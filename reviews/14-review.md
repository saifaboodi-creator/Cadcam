# Chapter 14 review: Zirconia

**Date:** 2026-11-22
**Method:** Three independent checkers (A, B, C) each checked the chapter, its 28 references and the three figures. A false-positive filter then re-checked every merged finding against the chapter text, the PubMed abstract and metadata of the cited paper, and the SVG source for figure findings. Evidence is limited to PubMed abstracts and metadata; full texts were not read.
