**Date:** 2026-11-22

## Discarded findings

1. **Ref 6 second author "Peričić TP" (C22).** The citation matches PubMed metadata (last name "Peričić", initials "TP"). Changing it to "Poklepović Peričić T" needs the publisher page, which is outside abstract and metadata checking.
2. **Ref 4 missing article number (C23).** PubMed gives no article number yet (volume 26, issue 1 only), and the notes file already flags it under "References to check". It is not a new error.
3. **18.4.4 "PEEK frameworks are metal-free, light and aesthetically less conspicuous" uncited (A19).** A correct standard material statement with no reference attached; the same paragraph cross-refers to Chapter 15. No factual error was found.
4. **Uncited statements in Table 18.1 and 18.1 (B21).** Printed-teeth wear (cross-refers to Chapter 11), "Low cost" and the 18.1 landmark point are general statements the notes file lists as such. No factual error was found; the terminology part of B21 is covered by #8.
5. **Cost claims in Table 18.1 and Clinical Tip 4 (A20).** Not an error. The only support offered is background text in ref 7 ("3D printing is more economical"), not a finding, and adding [7] to Table 18.1 would break first-citation order. Left as an editorial choice.

Sub-claims removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- B14 (merged into #21): the Hu [24] sentence "supported the defect during surgery and helped to prevent soft-tissue contracture and facial deformity" matches the abstract ("support of the maxillary defect during the surgery and minimized the incidence of cicatricial contracture of the soft tissue, preventing permanent facial deformity").
- C6 (merged into #19): the point that the study also measured three occlusal rests digitally. The chapter makes no claim about it.
- A9 and B3 (merged into #4): the point that ref 12's "low accuracy" undermines the Key Point. That issue is handled in #2, and ref 12 gives no comparison with casting.

## Top items for the author

1. **#3 (major): PEEK frameworks called "milled".** Ref 21 used indirect CAD/CAM production for both PEEK and Co-Cr frameworks; nothing in the abstract says milled. Fix 18.4.4, Table 18.2 and Table 18.3 (and Learning Objective 4, #7).
2. **#1 (major): Zandinejad "meta-analysis of six studies".** Only two of the six studies were pooled for retention.
3. **#2 (major): SLM "lower accuracy than casting" in Table 18.2.** Ref 12 calls SLM accuracy "low" without comparing it with casting; only binder jetting was compared.
4. **#4 and #5 (major): SLM fit and printed-pattern casting.** "At least as well as casting" ignores the poorer fit at 45° and 90° build orientations, and Table 18.2 says there are no printed-pattern data although refs 16 and 17 report them.
5. **#6 (major): Key Point on border-moulded impressions.** The retention advantage comes from one included study of maxillary bases and should be worded as the review's preference.
