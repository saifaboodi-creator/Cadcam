## Discarded findings

1. **13.8.3 bacterial adhesion: only one of two lithium disilicate products had the least adhesion (A15).** The chapter wording matches the authors' own conclusion (40819782: "lithium disilicate exhibited the least bacterial adhesion after UW aging"). The laboratory context ("after ultraviolet ageing… tested") is already stated.
2. **13.8.2 feldspathic p = 0.06 overstates the contrast (C21).** "Did not cause significant wear in that analysis" is accurate (41590161: "Feldspathic ceramics did not show significant enamel wear (p = 0.06…)"), and the authors' conclusion makes the same contrast.
3. **Table 13.3 "ZLS only at ≥0.75–1.0 mm thickness [19]" may imply 0.75 mm is enough (C28).** The wording matches the source (34415434: "Observation of an MMT of at least 0.75-1.0 mm for ZLS-ceramics is essential"). Section 13.5.3 already says that both groups were thinner than the manufacturer recommended.
4. **Fig 13.3 "Zirconia has no glassy phase: hydrofluoric acid and silane do not work on it" (B23).** This is correct standard knowledge, it is cross-referenced to Chapter 14, and it agrees with Ref 3 (12644802: "requires substantially different bonding methods than to silica-based ceramics").
5. **Uncited standard statements (B24, C25, C27, and A22b).** These cover the Table 13.1 aesthetics and bonding ratings, the Table 13.2 statement on conventional cements, the 13.7.2 etching principle and "over-etching weakens the surface", and the 13.2.2 list of feldspathic strengths. No factual error was found. Most are already logged in the notes file as uncited guidance, and colour stability is supported by Ref 6 in the next sentence. Adding sources or opinion labels is an editorial choice.
6. **Reference metadata gaps (B25).** Ref 20 has no article number and Ref 23 no volume or pages in PubMed. Neither is an error, and both are already listed in the notes file.

Sub-claims removed from findings that were otherwise confirmed:
- A10: the 13.5.2 sentence "a comparison of factory-crystallised chairside blocks… compared with… crystallised lithium disilicate". It already distinguishes lithium disilicate as crystallised.
- A23: the 13.7.1 text and Fig 13.3 caption citations [3,22]. Ref 22 supports HF plus silane as the accepted treatment, and Ref 3 supports well-documented bonding to silica-based ceramics. Only 13.1.2 is miscited (#9).
- B6, C26 and A22e: the 13.8.1 sentence "A rough surface increases plaque retention and staining…". It is an uncited standard statement with no factual error. Only the 13.8.2 "common to the literature" phrasing is confirmed (#22).
- A13, B17 and C16: "zirconia dissolved in the glassy phase" (13.5.1). It is uncited composition detail, not attached to Ref 15, and no error was found.
- A7 and C18: the "major" severity for Table 13.3 implant crowns (kept as minor, #23). C18's attrition point was moved to #20.

## Top items for the author

1. **#2 (major): Key Points "Limit them [feldspathic and leucite] … [8]".** Sailer et al. limit only feldspathic/silica-based crowns. They found leucite- and lithium disilicate-reinforced crowns similar to metal-ceramic, so the citation contradicts the source for leucite.
2. **#3 (major): FDP indications contradict each other.** 13.4.3 allows premolar three-unit FDPs, Table 13.1 says anterior only, and Table 13.3 says posterior FDPs are "not recommended [14]". Ref 14 advises only caution.
3. **#5 (major): self-adhesive cementation "acceptable for retentive crowns".** This rests on one single-arm cohort (41 crowns, 26 re-examined, one retention loss), which is not a basis for a general recommendation.
4. **#1 (major): feldspathic "lowest strength [5]".** This appears in four places. Ref 5 does not rank feldspathic last, and in Ref 9 leucite was the weakest.
5. **#4 and #6 (major): Table 13.1 and Key Points.** Table 13.1 calls lithium disilicate "highest of the group" next to a ~444 MPa ZLS value that beat it. The Key Points "smooth surfaces are kinder [21,24,25]" carries three citations that do not test surface finish.
