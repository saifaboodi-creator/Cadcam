## Discarded findings

1. **Figure 3.1, "high trueness, low precision" dots not centred (C).** This is a false positive. The six dots have a centroid of (500, 109.7), on the target centre (500, 110). The low-trueness clusters have centroids of (330, 86.5) and (726.7, 91), off the centre, as intended.
2. **Figure 3.2, unequal distortion between panels (C).** This is a false positive. The test line rises 50 px over 300 px in both panels (115→165 and 140→190), so the distortion is the same.
3. **Ref 10, "r = 0.77" (A).** The abstract gives r = 0.771 for both trueness and precision. The rounding is correct.
4. **Ref 27, "about 145–174 µm" (A).** The abstract gives 144.68–174.36 µm. The rounding is correct.
5. **Refs 1, 2 and 19 cannot be verified on PubMed (A).** They are already marked [UNVERIFIED] inline and listed in the notes file. This is not a new error. (The missing marker in Key Points is finding 17.)
6. **3.3.2, best-fit alignment hides systematic distortion, uncited (B).** This is already recorded in the notes file (Caveats). Not a new finding.
7. **3.4.3, original source of the 1° passive-fit threshold (B).** This is already recorded in the notes file. Ref 16's abstract uses the threshold ("the 1° threshold required for a passive fit"), and the chapter attributes it correctly.
8. **Ref 17 lacks a volume and pages (A).** PubMed (42946612) still shows no volume or pages. The online-first citation is correct today and is already in the notes file.
9. **Ref 25, "internal fit of CAD/CAM crowns" when measurement copings were used (A).** The abstract itself says "To measure the internal fit of the computer-aided designed/computer-aided manufactured (CAD/CAM) crowns". The chapter follows the source.
10. **3.4.2, "Digital systems fell in between" not stated in ref 4 (A).** The extremes of the range are both conventional (VSE 12.3 µm, ALG 167.2 µm), so the digital values must lie between them. The statement follows from the abstract.
11. **Ref 7 described as a systematic review although its title says "A Review" (A).** The abstract says "To conduct a systematic review", and PubMed indexes it as a Systematic Review.
12. **Ref 15 cited as 2026 although published online in 2025 (A).** The print issue is 2026 (135(5)), and the book cites by print year. The notes file records this.
13. **Ref 29 first author "da Silva SEG" vs PubMed "Silva SEGD" (A).** This is the same author. The chapter uses the usual Portuguese surname form, and the DOI matches.

## Top items for the author

1. **#3 (major): 3.7.1, Zheng endocrowns.** The chapter says glass-ceramics "fitted best" at 60 µm and composites at 120 µm. The source reports the best *agreement between the designed and measured gap* at those settings, which is a different result.
2. **#1 (major): 3.4.2, the 11.5–60.2 µm range.** This range covers all eight methods, conventional and digital, and one digital system (CEREC Bluecam) was among the most true and precise. The chapter attributes the range to conventional materials only.
3. **#2 (major): 3.6 lead-in.** "Two examples show how strongly the method affects the numbers" is followed by a trial (ref 25) that found no difference between methods.
4. **#4 (major): 3.8 checklist item 1.** "In vitro studies usually overestimate clinical accuracy [3,4]" is not supported by either abstract. Reword without the citation.
5. **#8, #12, #19–#21 (minor): in vitro labels.** Several in vitro results (operator training, photogrammetry, cement space, milling vs printing, crystallisation) are stated without the label STYLE.md requires.
